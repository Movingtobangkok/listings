Privacy Policy

Movingtobangkok CRM is a private tool used only by its owner, a Bangkok rental agent, to manage their own property listings, clients and appointments. It is not offered to the public.

When the owner connects their Google account, the tool uses Google Calendar to create viewing appointments and Google Sheets to copy the owner's listing and client lists into the owner's own spreadsheet. The data stays in the owner's own computer and Google account. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: via the Movingtobangkok Facebook page.
